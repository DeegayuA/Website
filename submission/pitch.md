## Pitch (~100 words — paste into the submission form)

A portfolio built from silicon to interface by one engineer. The name is the
canvas: a Navier–Stokes fluid simulation renders ink only through the glyphs
of a bilingual English/Sinhala wordmark — the same duality the work lives in.
Below it, nothing is decoration: commit heatmaps stream live from GitHub on a
6-hour cycle, and five researched case studies cover a 2 MW solar SCADA
platform, a battery-factory ERP, and an AI vision assistant for visually
impaired users. Monochrome, one accent moment, quality-tiered effects that
degrade gracefully — audited to Lighthouse 100 across accessibility, best
practices and SEO.
